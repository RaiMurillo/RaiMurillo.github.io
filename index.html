// approach

What's consistent across all four

01

Verify before writing

Pricing, ratings, materials, and claims are pulled from the live product listing first — nothing is asserted that isn't on the actual page.

02

Real photography

Hero and detail images come from the brand's own CDN, not stock substitutes or generated art standing in for the product.

03

Interactive by default

Shade pickers, colorway swatches, and lightboxes are built in vanilla JS — no framework dependency, plain HTML/CSS/JS throughout.
